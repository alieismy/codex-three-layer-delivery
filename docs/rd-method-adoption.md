## Current decision

Retain the current Skill instructions and use the scenario map below to select concrete methods for professional tasks. The current method-adoption decision supplied one real writing task. Its proxy reader could identify the recommended action, relevant responsibilities, next inputs, and evidence limits without a decision-blocking clarification. Keep that first draft and reader-use observation as a worked example; an extra instruction or regression is not justified by this result. Other methods remain available for representative tasks with the necessary inputs. An existing method does not have to fail before a reversible capability-improvement trial is worthwhile.

The intended reader is the current adoption decision-maker, who works across system design, architecture, requirements, research, standards, and technical writing. The decision is which transferable methods deserve use in those activities, not whether to import seven upstream frameworks. The [repository's users and deliverables](../README.md#who-this-is-for) and [nine independent responsibilities](../README.md#skill-reference-eight-specialists-and-one-explicit-orchestrator) control this scope.

Three options are real: retain the current methods; try one concrete method in a bounded professional deliverable; or expand all nine Skill instructions now. The second has a reversible path and can reveal useful refinements without assuming a general deficiency. Retaining current methods avoids new work but leaves the candidate's value unknown. Expanding every Skill now would duplicate existing principles before establishing professional benefit. These are decision judgments, not measured cost claims.

The strongest objection to another trial is that existing instructions are already substantial and earlier baselines satisfied every selected assertion. A further reading step costs model or human effort and can produce invalid requests for more detail. Its value must be a better decision or usable handoff, not extra text, questions, findings, or a higher number of method names.

## Writing trial and adoption decision

The frozen plan uses the current Chinese `rd-writing` body and decision-brief reference for an initial draft. A fresh proxy reader then receives only that draft and seven practical reading tasks. If source-checked feedback identifies a material explanation problem, the author revises it and a new proxy reader uses the revision. A genuine unresolved stakeholder decision remains unresolved. If there is no material problem, the baseline is retained without unnecessary calls.

The questions cover the recommended action, next inputs and responsibility, needs in three adjacent professional scenarios, the limits of historical evidence, the strongest objection, the conditions for changing a decision, and actual points requiring clarification. Results are assessed for professional correctness, decision value, downstream usability, and total effort. Headings, keyword counts, extra findings, and prose length do not earn credit.

The plan permits a before/after workflow observation only if a material correction warrants revision.
