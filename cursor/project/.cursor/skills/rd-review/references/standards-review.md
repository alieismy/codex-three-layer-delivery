# Standards Review

## Dimensions

- Authority, document status, scope, audience, and governing drafting rules
- Technical correctness and alignment with normative references
- Separation of mandatory, recommended, permitted, and informative content
- Verifiability, enforceability, terminology, symbols, figures, tables, and references
- Internal conflicts and conflicts with upstream law, policy, or standards
- Implementation neutrality unless the standard intentionally constrains implementation

## Failure Hypotheses

- A normative clause uses vague verbs or unverifiable adjectives
- A cited document’s title, status, applicability, or clause content is unverified
- Informative guidance is accidentally made mandatory, or vice versa
- An expert preference is presented as an authorized requirement

## Evidence

Identify exact clauses and authoritative sources. If normative text is inaccessible, review only verified metadata and mark content for human confirmation.
