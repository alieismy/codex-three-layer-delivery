# Technical Article Mode

Use for explanatory technical articles and white papers whose main purpose is understanding, evaluation, or informed adoption.

## Recommended Structure

1. Literal topic or decision-relevant title
2. Executive answer or abstract
3. Problem and scope
4. Mechanism or architecture
5. Evidence, examples, or comparative analysis
6. Strongest counterargument and limitations
7. Practical implications or recommendation
8. Sources and unresolved questions

Explain mechanisms before recommendations. For products, libraries, AI tools, and infrastructure, keep version, environment, capability layer, security, and operational constraints visible. Label constructed examples as examples.
