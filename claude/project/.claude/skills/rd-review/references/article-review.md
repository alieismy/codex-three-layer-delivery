# Article Review Mode

Use for technical articles, white papers, public-affairs articles, opinion pieces with factual claims, and fact-check reports.

## Evidence and Accuracy Axis

- Decompose central and supporting claims; classify fact, quotation, estimate, inference, prediction, opinion, and value judgment
- Verify names, dates, versions, chronology, units, denominator, quotation context, and source ownership
- Check whether each citation entails the adjacent claim and whether material omissions distort it
- Distinguish genuinely independent corroboration from multiple reports repeating one source
- Apply the same evidence standard regardless of agreement with the article's position

## Argument and Editorial Axis

- Identify the thesis, premises, inference steps, counterarguments, limitations, and conclusion
- Test causal claims for chronology, mechanism, confounders, selection effects, and alternative explanations
- Check for equivocation, category errors, false dilemmas, cherry-picking, overgeneralization, and certainty beyond evidence
- Assess audience fit, structure, terminology, and clarity only after factual and logical integrity
- Do not rewrite the author's position during independent review; propose bounded corrections and identify what must be re-sourced or removed

Use **Publishable / Publishable after revision / Not publishable** when publication is the decision. A central fabricated, materially false, or unverifiable claim presented as fact is normally Critical or Blocker depending on impact.
