# Decision Brief Mode

Use for concise decision memos, position papers, and executive technical briefs.

## Recommended Structure

1. Decision required
2. Recommendation and confidence
3. Decision criteria and constraints
4. Options and current-state baseline
5. Strongest objection
6. Evidence and trade-offs
7. Risks, reversibility, and exit path
8. Conditions, owner, and next verification step

Keep technical detail proportional to the audience's decision. Do not suppress a material downside to make the recommendation appear cleaner.
