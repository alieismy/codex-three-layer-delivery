# Feasibility Review

## Dimensions

- Decision question, scope, baseline, and alternatives
- Separation of facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns
- Technical, economic, schedule, operational, security, compliance, and risk coverage
- Cost and schedule estimation basis, sensitivity, and uncertainty
- Conditions, blockers, fallback options, and verification actions
- Whether the conclusion follows from evidence rather than preference

## Failure Hypotheses

- “Feasible” hides unresolved conditions or external dependencies
- A preferred option is compared against weak or missing alternatives
- Cost, schedule, maturity, policy, or standards claims are unsourced
- Risks are listed without changing the decision or verification plan

## Evidence

Use current authoritative sources for volatile claims and make unavailable evidence explicit.
