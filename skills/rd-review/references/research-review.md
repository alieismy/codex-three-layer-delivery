# Research Review Mode

Review research reports and technology, open-source, product, or AI-tool evaluations on two axes.

## Evidence Axis

- Research question, scope, date/version/jurisdiction, and stopping condition are explicit
- Material claims map to inspected sources with correct authority, currency, and applicability
- Search and selection methods do not systematically exclude counterevidence
- Repository activity, vendor claims, benchmarks, demos, and reproduced behavior are not conflated
- Conflicts, inaccessible sources, negative findings, and reproducibility limits are disclosed

## Decision-Quality Axis

- Evaluation criteria trace to the actual decision and include a relevant baseline
- Alternatives receive comparable evidence treatment
- Facts, estimates, inferences, and recommendations are separated
- Security, privacy, licensing, lifecycle, support, integration, and exit path are covered when material
- Recommendation strength and confidence do not exceed the evidence

Reject or require rework when source selection, unsupported central claims, or non-reproducible comparisons materially invalidate the recommendation.
